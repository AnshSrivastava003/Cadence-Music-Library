# Included demo media

The six SVG covers and six 24-second WAV instrumentals in the frontend assets directory were generated specifically for this project. They contain geometric vector artwork and synthesized musical tones, with no commercial recordings or downloaded cover artwork. No external media service is needed to play them.

Treat these as functional sample assets. Replace them with your own appropriately licensed recordings and artwork when personalizing the portfolio. The app accepts directly playable HTTPS media URLs as well as its local assets.
